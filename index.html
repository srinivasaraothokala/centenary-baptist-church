Faith & history

150 years of Emunah — faithfulness

Centenary Baptist Church is the first Baptist church planted in the Deccan region, born out of the "Lone Star Mission" carried by American Baptist missionaries to the Telugu-speaking people. We stand in the Baptist tradition of believer's baptism, a congregation gathered under Christ, and a life of self-supporting, self-governing ministry.

Our 150th-anniversary theme, Emunah (Hebrew for "faithfulness"), reflects our founding conviction — that the same God who sustained a handful of believers under a mission bungalow in 1875 has faithfully sustained this congregation for 150 years.

The story begins with S. Ethe Rajooloo, a pensioned soldier whose conversation with missionaries Rev. John E. Clough and Dr. Jewett at the 1874 Ongole Association meeting planted the idea of mission work in the Deccan. Rev. Woodley Williamson Campbell and his wife Lydia arrived in Secunderabad on 3 July 1875, held their first prayer meeting the next day, and began preaching in the open-air bazaar that August. On 14 November 1875, the Baptist Telugu Church, Secunderabad was formally organised with 14 believers.

From that small beginning, the congregation grew into the mother church of dozens of branch churches and outreach centres across Telangana. The present sanctuary's foundation stone was laid on the church's centenary, 14 November 1975, and the new sanctuary was dedicated on 17 February 1991. Rev. Dr. N. Thomas led the church for 24 years (1991–2015). Today, under Senior Pastor Rev. Dr. M. Purushotham, the church worships in Telugu, English, Hindi and Manipuri, and coordinates 47 active outreach centres working toward a vision of 150 outreach churches by the sesquicentennial.

Ministries & institutions

01

Medical Ministry

14 free medical camps this year, plus a weekly Sunday clinic for general medicine and dental care after the 9:30 AM Telugu service.

02

Centenary Baptist Church School

Started in 1992 with 35 children and 3 teachers; enrolment has now grown to over 500 students, with two new buildings and free education extended to 150 students.

Est. 1992, Picket, Secunderabad
03

Prayer Tower

A two-storey prayer building with a round cabin for individual prayer and a hall seating 100. Daily 10:30 AM–6:30 PM, plus Fasting Prayers every Friday and All-Night Prayers every 2nd Friday.

Est. 2003
04

William Carey Library

An air-conditioned, computerised Christian library of nearly 3,000 books, open to Christians and non-Christians alike.

Est. 2000 · Mon/Wed/Fri 9 AM–1 PM · Tue/Thu/Sat 1–7 PM
05

Baptist Bible Institute

A 10-month lay-training institute (Jan–Nov) offering a Certificate in Theology and Graduate in Theology, aiming to equip at least one theologically-trained member in every CBC family.

Dedicated 1 Aug 1995
06

Baptist Music School

Teaches Keyboard, Drums, Guitar, Violin, Flute, Saxophone & Vocals to an international syllabus (Trinity College London / Rock School London).

Classes Monday & Friday, 5–8 PM
07

Missions & Prayer

21 Days of Prayer for Missions, 150 Days of Prayer through the Psalms, and a growing outreach-church network sponsored by member families — 47 active centres and counting.

08

Women's Ministry

An outreach church made possible through the Women's Fellowship, and an annual Women's Retreat that has drawn as many as 800 attendees.

09

Sunday School & Youth

Palm Sunday procession, VBS teacher training for 80 teachers, a 4-day Teens Camp for 60 teens, and monthly Teens & Tweens meetings.
